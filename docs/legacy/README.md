# Historical materials

These files record the former programme, prototypes and marketing work. They are preserved as evidence of what existed; they do not describe the current Luci interface, deployment requirements, completed work or accepted scope. Start with [the current Luci plan](../luci/PLAN.md), [application contract](../luci/API.md) and [operator runbook](../RUNBOOK.md).

- [Prototype files](prototype/) retain the original HTML demonstrations, including fictional data and simulated outcomes.
- [Marketing materials](marketing/) retain their original branding, PDFs, assets and claims. They are not Luci launch materials and are not included in Luci release packages.
- [Former agent UX brief](AGENTIC-UX-panacea-2026-09.md) and [prototype QA notes](prototype-qa-panacea-2026-09.md) explain references in older code comments.
- [Former seams document](SEAMS-panacea-2026-09.md) preserves the phase-era integration index before its current-scope clarification.

The relocation on 2 October 2026 preserved the bytes of all 28 moved files; [the archive manifest](archive-2026-10-02.json) records their original and archived paths and SHA-256 hashes. Relative links between marketing HTML, styles, images and fonts retain their original layout. Historical `prototype/...` and `docs/marketing/...` path references in prose identify the former locations. The archives under `../superpowers/` and `../verification/` remain in place for their existing evidence references.

Archiving does not grant new rights to the material, its logos, fonts or third-party assets. Their original attribution and rights remain unchanged. Any public source/history export needs the ownership and licence review described in [the release guide](../luci/RELEASE.md).
