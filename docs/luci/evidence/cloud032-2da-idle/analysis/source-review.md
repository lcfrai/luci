The fixed measurement includes automation transport and two animation frames by design. No time is subtracted, no rows are discarded, and all failure thresholds stay unchanged.

Both Home card memoization and first-open discussion presentation are included in 2da and have separate functional/source regression evidence. This run does not establish a latency benefit from either; it also does not establish a single new product bottleneck justifying another blind component change. A bounded diagnostic with browser CPU/GC/style/layout tracing that overlaps an actual primary tail, while retaining the unmodified reference collector and labeling tracing overhead, would give stronger attribution. It must be separate from acceptance and cannot be used to subtract overhead to declare a pass. The sustained mixed-workload and its latency gate remain necessary independently.
