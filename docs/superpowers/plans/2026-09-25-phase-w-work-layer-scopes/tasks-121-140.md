**122 — insights queries: measured counts and durations, percentiles for the scatter.** Stream WH.
Files: new `store/work_insights.go`, `server/work_insights.go` (+ tests), ⚑
`server/matrix_integration_test.go`. Route (SW.20's `QueryInsights`): `POST
/v1/teams/{teamId}/insights/query` with `InsightsQuery {teamId, projectId?, from, to, measure:
count|cycle_time|lead_time|triage_time, groupBy?}` → `InsightsResult {groups [{key, count}], points
[{issue, ms}], p50Ms, p75Ms, p90Ms}`. **Every number is read from recorded state moves** (task 10's
`StateMove` events, the Hub's `at`, never the client's clock), never an estimate (Q1, `OD-27`):
`cycle_time` is the first move into `started` to the standing `done`; `lead_time` is creation to the
standing `done`; `triage_time` is creation in `triage` to the first move out; an issue reopened and
not now `done` has no duration; `groupBy` is one of `state`, `assignee`, `label`, `feature`,
`cycle`, `priority`, `criterion` (an issue serving two criteria counts once in each group, and the
result says so in its `groups` keys, never as a share). Percentiles are **nearest rank** over the
points and `null` when there are none. Visibility is the caller's: the team's own projects, plus a
project granted to the caller's team (task 117's `workAccess`); erased issues still count (their
ids are kept, §3.6). A range is bounded (`from < to`, at most 366 days, else `422`). **Two gaps in
SW.20, listed here and fixed in 114, not in this task:** L-150's *effort* needs a
`measure: estimate_sum` over recorded estimates (task 79), which 114's enum lacks; L-151's scatter
needs each point's `at` (the moment its duration ended), which 114's `points [{issue, ms}]` lacks.
Until 114 adds both, this task serves the rest and the part says what is missing. Tests first, each
with its mutant: an issue moved `triage → todo → started → in_review → landed → done` has cycle time
`done.at − started.at` (kills "from creation"); an issue closed by hand and never started is in
`count` and in no duration (kills "a zero-length point"); points of 1 to 10 hours give 5, 8 and 9
hours (kills "interpolated" and "mean"); no closed issue gives `points: []` and three `null`s (kills
"p50 of 0"); another team's issue is never counted, a granted project's is (kills "visibility not
applied"); the response JSON carries no key named like `forecast`, `eta`, `trend`, `expected` or
`remaining` (kills "a prediction in the handler", beside 114's type check); `from ≥ to` and a
400-day range are `422` (kills "unbounded scan"); a two-criterion issue is one point, not two
(kills "double-counted durations"). Depends on 116. Design: §1 K (L-150, L-151), §5.4 W4 (the
insights row), Q1, AX Principle 7. Closes **L-150** (less effort) and **L-151** with 132. Does NOT
claim dashboards (L-152, task 177), a surface on the rail or the Desk (never, Q1), or query speed
at any size (unmeasured).
